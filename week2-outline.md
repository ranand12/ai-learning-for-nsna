# Week 2 · Use AI better

**Goal:** move from "asking a chatbot questions" to getting great results, and knowing which tool to use for each job.
**Outcome:** someone who stops after Week 2 can learn, research, verify, reuse and delegate with AI, and pick the right tool for the job.
**Format:** about 4 hours. Every stop follows the same pattern: big idea → slides → live demo.

## Part 1 · Get better results

### Stop 1 · Ask better (25 min)
**Big idea:** the AI only knows what's on the desk, so brief it like a new colleague.
1. **It can't read your mind**: a vague prompt goes in and generic slop comes out (mind-reader GIF).
2. **The brief**: four cards drop onto the desk: 🎯 Goal · 📎 Context · 🚧 Constraints · 📄 Output format.
3. **Show, don't describe**: one example beats a paragraph of instructions.
4. **"Ask me questions first"**: 💃 replies with "?" pills before it answers.
5. **No magic prompt**: v1 → v2 → v3 on a loop, and the output gets better each time.

**Demo:** take a weak prompt and upgrade it live in three rounds.

### Stop 2 · Learn better (25 min)
**Big idea:** turn the AI into a patient tutor that works with your own material.
1. **Explain at my level**: a depth dial on the same topic: age 5 → 15 → expert.
2. **Quiz me**: the arrows reverse, so 💃 asks the questions and you answer.
3. **What am I missing?**: it finds the gaps in your understanding.
4. **Beyond "summarize this"**: a messy pack turns into an FAQ · timeline · study guide · action items.
5. **Tool card: Gemini Notebook** (formerly NotebookLM): learn from *your* PDFs, videos and notes. 🃏 → toolbox.

**Demo:** learn an unfamiliar topic in Gemini Notebook, then explain it back in your own words.

### Stop 3 · Research better (25 min)
**Big idea:** know which of the four ways you're using, and verify anything important.
1. **Four ways to find out**, a ladder:
   - from memory (frozen 💃, has a cutoff)
   - web search 🔍
   - AI search
   - deep research (🕵️ goes off for minutes)
2. **Tool cards: Perplexity** (AI search) and **Deep Research** (long cited reports). 🃏🃏 → toolbox.
3. **Plan first**: ask for a research plan before asking for answers.
4. **Fact · inference · opinion**: claims are sorted into three buckets, like the Week 1 closed/open sort.
5. **Citations can lie**: check important claims at the source.

**Demo:** the same question in chat, then Perplexity, then Deep Research. Compare the time taken, the sources and the depth.

### Stop 4 · Think & create better (25 min)
**Big idea:** use AI to stress-test your thinking, then to polish what you make.
1. **Brainstorm, then judge**: many ideas → a scored shortlist.
2. **Devil's advocate**: "What's the strongest argument against my plan?"
3. **Pre-mortem**: "It's 6 months later and this failed. Why?"
4. **Two 💃s, two jobs**: one generates and the other critiques.
5. **Rough → polished**: the same notes rewritten for a 10-year-old and for a CEO.
6. **Tool card: Google AI Studio**: a playground for trying ideas and quick prototypes. 🃏 → toolbox.

**Demo:** work through a real decision, with one model generating and another critiquing.

## Part 2 · Pick the right tool

### Stop 5 · Specialists vs all-rounders (15 min)
**Big idea:** a specialist doctor vs your family doctor. All-rounders handle anything, and specialists do one job brilliantly.
1. **Two families**: the stage splits. 🩺 Specialists on the left (Gemini Notebook, Perplexity, Deep Research, AI Studio, Claude Code/Codex). 💃 All-rounders on the right (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini). The toolbox cards fly to their side.
2. **How they differ**: specialists give the best result for their one job but are useless outside it. All-rounders are flexible, but you have to steer them more.
3. **Specialist vs all-rounder, head to head**: Gemini Notebook vs ChatGPT with an uploaded file. Gemini Notebook stays strictly inside your sources and points to where each answer came from. Chat mixes in what it already knows.

### Stop 6 · Make it yours: Projects + Skills (20 min)
**Big idea:** stop repeating yourself. Set things up once and reuse them.
1. **Stop living in 300 chats**: a messy sidebar → tidy project folders (Projects / Gems / custom instructions).
2. **Handoff summary**: Ghajini again. Before a new chat, ask for a summary so you don't start from zero.
3. **The reuse ladder**: prompt → saved template → **skill** → agent.
4. **Introducing Skills**: a shelf of recipe cards beside the desk. When a task comes in, only the matching card slides onto the desk, so the desk stays clean.
   - It differs from a saved prompt because the AI picks the right skill itself.
   - You'll see it as Claude Skills, custom GPTs and Gemini Gems.

**Demo:** a "write it in my style" skill. Ask the same thing with and without it.

### Stop 7 · Give it hands: agent levels + MCP (35 min)
**Big idea:** all-rounders can work at different levels. Each step up, the 🕵️ gets more access, you watch it less, and there's more risk.
**The same task at every level:** *"Do my monthly expense report."*

| Level | Where it works | Example | Expense report |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0 · Chat** | The chat window | ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini | Tells you the steps. You do it all |
| **1 · Chat + tools** | A sandbox in the chat | Same, with file upload | Receipt photos in → spreadsheet out |
| **2 · Coworker** | Your connected apps + folders | **ChatGPT Work**, **Claude Cowork** | Reads Receipts folder + Gmail, builds the sheet, drafts the email |
| **3 · Takes over your Mac** | Your actual screen | Computer Use (Cowork / ChatGPT Work) | Clicks through the old company expense site |
| **4 · Its own cloud computer** | A cloud computer, always on | OpenAI dots, Cowork scheduled tasks, Manus | Does it on the 1st of every month with your laptop closed, then asks "Approve?" |

1. **The ladder**: the 🕵️ climbs one level per click: 💬 → 📦 → 📁 → 🖥️ → ☁️.
2. **Levels 0–2**: one slide each, showing the request, what it can reach, and the result.
3. **Introducing MCP (connectors)**: "How does it get into my Gmail?"
   - It's like USB-C: one standard plug for every app. *Before:* a tangle of cables. *After:* one port. The 🕵️ plugs into Gmail · Drive · Calendar · Slack.
   - In the apps it's called "Connectors". This links back to Week 1's "Give it tools".
4. **Level 3**: "No connector? Then it clicks through the screen like you would."
5. **Level 4**: its own computer in the cloud.
6. **Your Mac vs a cloud computer**:
   - Mac: reaches your files and company sites, but must stay awake.
   - Cloud: works while you sleep, but needs your logins.
7. **Agent = model + tools + skills + loop**: the Week 1 spy formula, updated. The 🔌 MCP and 🃏 skill cards land on the spy.
8. **The trade-off**: 🔑 access ↑ · 😴 how much you watch it ↓ · ⚠️ risk ↑.
   - ✋ It should ask before spending money, sending to people or deleting.
   - Only connect apps you trust, and remember a webpage can trick it.

**Demo:** one task, three levels: chat → Cowork/Work on a folder → a scheduled cloud task.

### Stop 8 · Which tool when (20 min)
**Big idea:** start from the job, not the tool.
1. **The map**: the toolbox tips out and every tool lands on its job, one per click:

| I want to… | Use | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Ask, draft, brainstorm, rewrite | All-rounder chat | Fast, flexible, good enough for most things |
| Learn from **my own** material | Gemini Notebook | Stays inside your sources |
| A quick answer on something **current** | Perplexity | Live web, sources, seconds |
| A **deep report** | Deep Research | Hundreds of pages, cited, takes minutes |
| Do it **my way** every time | A skill | A saved recipe the AI picks up itself |
| Work across **my apps and files** | ChatGPT Work / Claude Cowork + connectors | Reaches your stuff |
| A site with **no connector** | Computer Use (level 3) | Clicks like you would |
| **Recurring** or overnight | Cloud agent (level 4) | Laptop can be closed |
| Try out a prototype | Google AI Studio | Playground |
| Write code | Claude Code / Codex | → Week 3 |

2. **The flowchart**, one question per click:
   - My own material? → Gemini Notebook
   - Fresh info? → Perplexity (quick) or Deep Research (deep)
   - Needs to *do* things? → Coworker → no connector: level 3 → recurring: level 4
   - Otherwise → chat
3. **Quiz: "Which tool?"**: six real tasks. The audience calls out the tool, then each task flies to it.
